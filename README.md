# 🛍️ Retail Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Dashboard** is an interactive **Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze multi-year retail sales performance.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of:

- 💰 Sales
- 📈 Profit
- 👥 Customers
- 🛒 Orders
- 📦 Products
- 🌎 Regions
- 🏙️ Cities
- 🏷️ Brands
- 📢 Sales Channels
- 🗂️ Categories

The project transforms cleaned retail sales data into an attractive and interactive dashboard using **KPIs, charts, tables, and slicers**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- 📌 Monitor overall retail sales performance.
- 💰 Track total sales and total profit.
- 👥 Analyze customer performance.
- 🛒 Analyze order performance.
- 📦 Analyze product performance.
- 🌎 Compare sales and profit across regions.
- 🏙️ Analyze sales performance by city.
- 🏷️ Analyze brand performance across cities.
- 📢 Compare sales across different channels.
- 🗂️ Compare sales, profit, and quantity by category.
- 🔎 Provide interactive filtering for business analysis.

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## 💳 KPI Cards

The dashboard contains five major Key Performance Indicators:

### 1. 💰 Total Sales
Shows the overall sales generated from retail transactions.

### 2. 📈 Total Profit
Shows the total profit generated from sales.

### 3. 👥 Total Customers
Shows the total number of customers included in the analysis.

### 4. 🛒 Total Orders
Shows the total number of orders.

### 5. 📦 Total Products
Shows the total number of products represented in the dataset.

---

## 📈 Charts and Visualizations

### 1. 🌎 Total Sales by Region

Shows the comparison of total sales across different regions.

**Purpose:**
- Compare regional sales.
- Identify regional performance differences.
- Understand sales contribution by region.

---

### 2. 🏷️ Total Sales by Brand and City

Shows sales performance for different brands across cities.

**Purpose:**
- Compare brands.
- Analyze city-level performance.
- Identify strong brand and city combinations.

---

### 3. 💵 Total Profit by Region

Shows the profit contribution of each region.

**Purpose:**
- Compare regional profitability.
- Understand profit distribution.
- Identify regions requiring further analysis.

---

### 4. 📢 Total Sales by Channel

Shows sales generated through different sales channels.

**Purpose:**
- Compare channel performance.
- Understand sales distribution.
- Analyze channel contribution to total sales.

---

### 5. 📦 Total Sales by Product ID

Shows sales performance at the product level.

**Purpose:**
- Identify high-sales products.
- Compare product performance.
- Support product-level analysis.

---

### 6. 📋 Category Performance Table

The table provides a detailed comparison of:

| Category | Total Sales | Total Profit | Total Quantity |
|----------|-------------|--------------|----------------|

**Purpose:**
- Compare category sales.
- Compare category profitability.
- Analyze quantity sold by category.

---

# 🎛️ Interactive Slicers

The dashboard contains five interactive slicers:

### 1. 🌎 Region
Filter the dashboard by region.

### 2. 🏙️ City
Filter the dashboard by city.

### 3. 🗂️ Category
Filter the dashboard by product category.

### 4. 📅 Year
Filter the dashboard by year.

### 5. 📅 Month
Filter the dashboard by month.

These slicers allow users to perform **dynamic and interactive analysis**.
